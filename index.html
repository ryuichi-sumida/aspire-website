UMCS

Understanding Multilingual Communication Spaces

UMCS is a UK–Japan research project developing real-time translation and learning technologies that bridge spoken and signed languages, starting with British Sign Language (BSL), Japanese Sign Language (JSL), English, and Japanese. Our goal is equitable, accessible communication for Deaf and hearing people alike, across languages and cultures.

Our Principles

To truly support Deaf communities, AI needs to recognize not just signs — but the people who are using them collaboratively.

Sign languages in their own right

Sign languages and multimodal resources are full scientific objects — not simplified stand-ins for spoken language.

Communication is co-constructed

Meaning is built in the moment — through handshape, gaze, touch, and space, with vocabulary invented on the fly and misunderstandings repaired as they go.

Deaf communities as partners

Deaf researchers and community members help decide what gets studied, how, and what the results mean — as co-researchers, advisors, and decision-makers, from the start.

Technology built for sign

AI and corpus tools built for the real structure of sign and tactile interaction — not retrofitted from speech pipelines.
